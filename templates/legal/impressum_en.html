Purpose of the site

diytracker.ch is a non-commercial, community-curated event calendar for DIY, rock, metal and punk concerts in Switzerland. The platform aggregates event data from publicly accessible sources (e.g. metalgigs.ch, petzi.ch) and supplements it with manually submitted contributions from invited contributors.
